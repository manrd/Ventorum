## V9. Sign conventions

Rolling moment due to sideslip of a rectangular wing (AR 10, alpha 4 deg). Standard convention: positive dihedral gives Cl_beta < 0. The dihedral term (Cl_beta minus the value of the flat wing) changes sign with the dihedral. In the vortex-lattice method the flat wing has a Cl_beta of its own: the Kutta-Joukowski force on the chordwise vortex legs that lie on the surface is not zero in sideslip. The lifting line has no chordwise extent (forces on the bound vortex only), so with the body-axis wake its flat-wing value is zero. With the free-stream wake the wake skew adds a further term.
