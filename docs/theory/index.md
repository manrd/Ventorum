# Theory manual

Ventorum uses the name Lanchester–Prandtl lifting-line theory, to credit both F. W. Lanchester and L. Prandtl.

The theory manual gives, for each model, the equations, the assumptions, the envelope of validity and the references, with credit to the authors of the methods.

:::{note}
The chapters are written after the recovery audit of the original solver family, so that they describe the models that stay in Ventorum. Until then, this page lists the planned chapters and their main references.
:::

| Chapter | Model | Main references |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Classical lifting line | Glauert's Fourier series solution of the Lanchester–Prandtl lifting-line equation | F. W. Lanchester, *Aerodynamics* (1907); L. Prandtl (1918-1919); H. Glauert, *The Elements of Aerofoil and Airscrew Theory* (1926) |
| Numerical lifting line | Linear and nonlinear lifting line with section polars | W. F. Phillips and D. O. Snyder, *Journal of Aircraft* 37(4), 2000 |
| Vortex lattice | Horseshoe vortices, chordwise panels, Trefftz-plane drag | J. Katz and A. Plotkin, *Low-Speed Aerodynamics*; M. Drela, *Flight Vehicle Aerodynamics* |
| Ground effect | Method of images over flat ground, height and bank derivatives, Irodov criterion | K. V. Rozhdestvensky, *Aerodynamics of a Lifting System in Extreme Ground Effect* |
| Forces and moments | Kutta-Joukowski near-field forces, Trefftz-plane induced drag, section moments | Katz and Plotkin; Drela |
| Trust score and error bars | Envelope checks, numerical and model-form uncertainty | W. L. Oberkampf and C. J. Roy, *Verification and Validation in Scientific Computing*; ASME V&V 20 |
